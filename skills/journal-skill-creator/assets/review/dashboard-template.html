Anchors / 4
4 no material issue; 3 minor revision; 2 substantive revision; 1 major reconstruction; 0 fails its purpose.
Calculation
100 × sum(weight × rating / 4) / applicable weight; round once to a whole point out of 100, then divide by 10.
Unknown (U)
Retain unknown weight. Interval endpoints assign it zero or full credit; coverage shows assessed applicable weight. This is not a statistical confidence interval. With no assessed dimensions, show Not assessed.
Not applicable
N/A excludes that dimension's weight; explain why it does not apply.
Bands
9.0–10 ready within assessed scope; 7.5–8.9 minor revision; 5.0–7.4 substantive revision; below 5.0 major revision.
Blocker
A demonstrated central contradiction or unsupported inference overrides the numeric band. Unavailable evidence alone is provisional, never a blocker.
Reassessment
No manuscript average. Selection does not revise text or change ratings. Higher scores require reassessment after changes. Apply the full scientific checks in the local rubric before assigning full credit.
